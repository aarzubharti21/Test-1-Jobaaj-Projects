## Amazon E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

# Project Overview

The rapid growth of e-commerce has transformed how businesses interact with customers and manage operations. Every online transaction generates valuable information that can help organizations improve sales performance, optimize inventory, streamline logistics, and enhance customer satisfaction. However, raw transactional data alone provides limited value unless it is converted into meaningful insights through analysis and visualization.

This project presents an interactive Amazon E-Commerce Sales Dashboard developed entirely in Microsoft Excel. The dashboard transforms large-scale transactional data into an executive reporting solution that enables business leaders to monitor revenue, customer behavior, fulfillment efficiency, product performance, and operational effectiveness.

Using Excel's analytical capabilities such as Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI Cards, Slicers, Conditional Formatting, and advanced formulas, the dashboard delivers an intuitive decision-support system that helps identify growth opportunities and operational bottlenecks.

The project follows the business objectives outlined in the accompanying problem statement, which emphasizes sustainable growth through data-driven decision-making across finance, customers, logistics, products, and service quality.

# Business Background

Modern e-commerce businesses process thousands of customer orders every day across multiple cities, product categories, fulfillment methods, and sales channels. Managing these operations efficiently requires continuous monitoring of key business metrics.

To overcome these challenges, the organization aims to leverage data analytics to optimize its operations and support sustainable business growth.

# Dashboard Overview

The Excel dashboard provides a centralized business reporting solution that enables users to monitor operational performance through interactive reports.

Users can filter data dynamically using slicers and instantly view changes across all KPI cards and visualizations.

The dashboard is designed to support executives, operations managers, supply chain teams, and business analysts.

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard summarizes business performance using executive KPI cards.

Key indicators include:

**Total Orders** -   Represents the total number of customer orders processed.
**Total Revenue** -    Displays the overall revenue generated from completed transactions.
**Average Order Value** -   Measures the average revenue generated per customer order.
**Total Quantity Sold** -   Represents the total units sold.
**Delivered Orders** -   Tracks successfully delivered customer orders.
**Cancelled Orders** -    Measures cancelled transactions.
**Pending Orders** -    Shows orders currently under processing.
**Average Quantity per Order** -   Evaluates customer purchasing behavior.

# Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains multiple interactive reports.

**Customer Insights Dashboard**

Examines top-performing locations, analyzes order cancellation patterns, and compares B2B versus B2C customer behavior to support targeted marketing decisions.

**Logistics & Fulfillment Dashboard**

Compares Amazon and Merchant fulfillment methods, evaluates shipping performance, and helps identify opportunities to reduce delivery delays and improve operational efficiency.

**Product Management Dashboard**

Identifies high-demand product categories, analyzes order quantities, and supports inventory optimization to minimize stock shortages and excess inventory.

**Customer Satisfaction Dashboard**

Studies order cancellations and returns, identifies products requiring quality improvements, and supports better customer service strategies.
