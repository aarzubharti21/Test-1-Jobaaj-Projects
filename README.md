## Amazon E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## Overview

I am excited to present the **Amazon Sales Performance Dashboard**, an interactive business intelligence project developed to transform raw Amazon order data into meaningful business insights. This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance, order fulfillment, product demand, regional distribution, and operational efficiency through dynamic visualizations and interactive filters. The objective of this project is to enable stakeholders to monitor business performance, identify growth opportunities, and make data-driven decisions using a centralized reporting solution.

## Problem Statement

The primary objective of this project was to build a comprehensive sales monitoring dashboard using Amazon e-commerce transactional data. The dataset contains thousands of order records with information related to product categories, order status, fulfillment methods, shipping locations, quantities, and revenue. Analyzing this large volume of data manually makes it difficult to identify business trends, operational bottlenecks, and customer demand patterns. Therefore, an interactive dashboard was developed to simplify sales analysis and provide actionable insights for business decision-making.

## Analysis Done

> The file in which the analysis is done is `Amazon Sales Dashboard.lookerstudio`

A systematic data preparation and business intelligence workflow was implemented to build an accurate and interactive dashboard. The analytical process included:

- **Data Cleaning:** Cleaned the dataset by handling missing values, removing duplicate records, correcting inconsistent data types, and validating order information to ensure reliable reporting.

- **Data Preparation:** Organized transactional data including Order ID, Date, Product Category, SKU, Quantity, Revenue, Fulfillment Method, Courier Status, Sales Channel, Shipping State, City, B2B Orders, and Promotion IDs for analysis.

- **Data Transformation:** Standardized date formats, created calculated metrics, categorized business dimensions, and prepared fields for interactive reporting and trend analysis.

- **Sales Performance Analysis:** Evaluated total sales, revenue, quantity sold, and order volume across multiple business dimensions to identify high-performing products and categories.

- **Order Status Analysis:** Analyzed delivered, shipped, cancelled, pending, and returned orders to evaluate order fulfillment performance and operational efficiency.

- **Regional Analysis:** Examined sales performance across states and cities to identify the strongest geographical markets and business expansion opportunities.

- **Product Category Analysis:** Compared product categories and SKUs to determine best-selling products and customer purchasing preferences.

- **Fulfillment Performance:** Evaluated merchant fulfillment versus Amazon fulfillment performance to monitor logistics efficiency and customer service quality.

- **Interactive Dashboard Development:** Designed an interactive Looker Studio dashboard with dynamic filters, KPI scorecards, charts, maps, and drill-down functionality for enhanced business exploration.

## Dashboard

The final deliverable consists of a highly interactive Looker Studio dashboard that enables users to analyze Amazon sales performance from multiple business perspectives.

- **Sales Overview Dashboard:** Displays key performance indicators such as **Total Revenue**, **Total Orders**, **Total Quantity Sold**, **Average Order Value**, **Product Categories**, and Overall Business Performance.

- **Category Performance Dashboard:** Provides a comparison of product categories based on sales, quantity sold, and revenue contribution to identify the highest-performing product segments.

- **Regional Sales Dashboard:** Visualizes state-wise and city-wise sales performance, helping identify major revenue-generating locations and regional demand patterns.

- **Order Status Dashboard:** Monitors order lifecycle including delivered, cancelled, pending, shipped, and returned orders to evaluate operational efficiency.

- **Fulfillment Dashboard:** Compares fulfillment methods and courier performance to identify opportunities for improving logistics and customer satisfaction.

- **Time-Series Analysis:** Tracks sales trends over time, allowing users to identify seasonal demand, business growth, and monthly sales fluctuations.

- **Interactive Filters:** Users can dynamically analyze the dashboard using filters for Date, Category, State, City, Sales Channel, Fulfillment Method, Courier Status, Order Status, and B2B Orders.

## Recommendations

Based on the interactive analysis performed in this dashboard, the following business recommendations are suggested:

- **Focus on High-Revenue Categories:** Increase inventory investment and promotional campaigns for product categories consistently generating the highest revenue.

- **Improve Order Fulfillment Efficiency:** Analyze cancelled and pending orders to identify operational issues and reduce delivery delays.

- **Strengthen Regional Sales Strategy:** Expand marketing efforts in high-performing states while introducing targeted campaigns in underperforming regions.

- **Optimize Inventory Management:** Forecast demand using historical sales trends to minimize stock shortages and reduce excess inventory.

- **Enhance Logistics Performance:** Improve courier partnerships and fulfillment processes to increase delivery speed and customer satisfaction.

- **Leverage Promotional Campaigns:** Evaluate promotion effectiveness and allocate marketing budgets toward campaigns producing higher sales conversion.

- **Adopt Data-Driven Business Decisions:** Utilize dashboard insights to support pricing strategy, inventory planning, demand forecasting, and overall business growth.

The dashboard showcases the ability to convert large-scale transactional datasets into meaningful business insights that support strategic decision-making and improve organizational performance.
